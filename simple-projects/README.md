# Simple projects

Small finished pieces — a title card, a lower third, a logo sting, a captioned clip. Each has a beginning and an end and could drop straight into a real edit, but stays readable enough to take apart in a few minutes.

Typical shape: one scene, a handful of layers, under about fifteen seconds.
